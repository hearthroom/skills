# Rituals for the agent: self-review, re-checks, written-out reasoning

Skills do not tell the agent to run a self-review pass, double-check or
re-verify its work, fill a reflection form, or write its reasoning into the
reply. The validator rejects the common phrasings.

## Why

Current models check their own work without being asked; instructions to do
it again add cost and no quality, and requests to reproduce reasoning in the
reply can be declined outright. Ceremony the agent has to carry also crowds
out the decision the skill exists to help with.

Checks on the card are different and stay: `card check`, the preview,
validation and a playtest test the card. Name them as checks on the card.

## Where it came up

- The slimming pass removed route rituals, packet forms, self-review sections
  and "hand off and stop" endings from 43 skills with no loss in a draft-only
  eval, commit `22cafcd`.
- Twelve "self-review" sections and steps left in references were renamed to
  checks on the card when the validator rule landed.
