# Fill-in templates and answer-shaped packets

The toolkit does not ship a card template to fill, packets that list answers
instead of decisions, or a required set of sections every definition must
have.

## Why

A card that can be produced by filling in a template is the generic card, and
the scorecard fails it. Templates also pull a capable agent toward completing
the form rather than finding what this card needs. Packets list the decisions
to make; the answers come from the premise.

## Where it came up

- A 15-section definition template and duplicated packets were removed in the
  rewrite, commit `32db74c`.
- "Questions, not a mould" became the toolkit's opening line, commit `75cd5de`.
