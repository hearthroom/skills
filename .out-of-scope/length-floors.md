# Minimum lengths

Skills do not set a minimum length for a definition, an opening or a reply,
or treat the validator's limits as a size to fill.

## Why

Length follows what the card needs. A floor makes the agent pad, and padding
dilutes the parts the model should attend to. Limits are ceilings. Languages
other than English keep a buffer below the ceiling, which is a different
thing from a floor.

## Where it came up

- A "5,000 to 10,000 characters" floor for definitions was removed in the
  rewrite, commit `32db74c`.
