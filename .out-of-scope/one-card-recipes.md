# One card's recipe written as a rule for every card

A layout, font treatment, animation, scene list or workaround that solved one
card's problem is not added to a skill as general guidance.

## Why

What worked for one card was shaped by that card's art, genre, language and
screen. Written as a rule, it becomes the default every later card copies,
which is the opposite of what the toolkit is for: the obvious version of a
card is the one to name and then move past. It also grows the skill for every
reader to serve one. The card's own `README.md` dossier keeps the recipe with
the evidence for it, where the next session on that card will find it.

What does belong in a skill is the principle the recipe taught, in a sentence,
when it would change how a different card is made. If the lesson is really a
fact about the platform (a crop, a browser quirk on the chat page), it goes in
`platform-facts.md`.
