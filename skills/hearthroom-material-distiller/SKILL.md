# Hearthroom Material Distiller

Turn a large source into a source-to-play map the next skill can draft from
without rereading the material. A short map that keeps agency, consequence,
voice and a first action beats a digest. The output is a map, not a card.

## Required references

Read `../../references/material-distillation.md` (passes, keep/delay/cut,
world and character compression, conflict handling). Read
`../../references/voice-calibration.md` when the material has dialogue or a
cast, `../../references/originality-adaptation.md` when it is canon-like,
and `../../references/cost-and-boundaries.md` when it is mature or personal.

## Workflow

1. List the sources and confirm the author wants them used; inspect only
   what the requested card needs. Inventory each by what it can contribute,
   without copying it.
2. Extract the playable promise: fantasy, player role, central tension, and
   the pressure that starts the first scene.
3. Keep a fact only if it creates agency, consequence, character behaviour,
   state, a route seed, voice or a first-scene action. Check every
   real-world fact against a reliable source before it enters a field; mark
   the unverified as assumptions.
4. Compress a world into modules (core rule, player position, locations,
   factions, state, route seeds, one opening problem) and a character into
   desire, contradiction, boundary, player leverage, voice, turn behaviour,
   progression. Mark every item keep, delay, cut, merge, rename or
   assumption.
5. Map what is kept to the folder: durable rules and engine to
   `definition.md`; the first scene to `welcome.md`; background that matters
   only once named to `lorebook.json` entries with keywords and descriptive
   names; visible status or layout ideas to a presentation note; one
   ordinary-turn sample for `talkExample`. Plan characters per field from
   `tokenBudget.limits`; cut history before agency, consequence, voice or
   state.
6. Ask only when the answer changes player role, intensity, central
   relationship, first scene or ownership.

## Packet

Inventory; playable promise; source-to-play map (definition | opening |
Lorebook | presentation | examples); delay / cut / merge / assumptions;
facts checked; character plan per field; missing author input. Continue
with `hearthroom-world-engineer` when it is still a world without playable
rules, `hearthroom-originality-adapter` for canon-like or renamed-copy risk,
`hearthroom-card-blueprint` when the concept still needs ideation, or
`hearthroom-card-author` when fields can be drafted.

## Do not

- Do not paste source passages into the card; rewrite as original rules and
  beats.
- Do not keep names, factions or mechanics that do not change play.
