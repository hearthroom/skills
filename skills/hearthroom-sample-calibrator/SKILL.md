# Hearthroom Sample Calibrator

Compare a draft to the closest synthetic sample by structure and name the
missing craft layer. The output is a calibration packet, not final fields
and not permission to copy sample prose.

## Required references

Read `../../references/sample-driven-calibration.md` and
`../../examples/sample-card-packets.md` to pick the closest sample. Read
`../../references/quality-scorecard.md` when the author asks whether the
calibrated draft is strong enough.

## Workflow

1. Classify the request: example-seeking, draft comparison, or copy-risk
   review.
2. Pick one primary sample and, only for a specific missing layer, one
   overlay; never average several samples into a hybrid.
3. Compare by structure: primary contract, promise surface, durable engine,
   opening proof, voice proof, whether the sample's example shows an
   ordinary turn, longplay proof, UI role, field allocation, play probes,
   originality.
4. State exactly which structure may be borrowed (field allocation,
   pressure shape, player agency, opening proof, probe type) and run the
   copy-risk check: names, places, artifacts, scene sentences, dialogue
   rhythm, tag sets, summary structure with nouns swapped, route or schema
   labels.
5. Name the missing layer and the repair order, then continue with
   `hearthroom-premise-workshop` (no premise yet),
   `hearthroom-archetype-director` (type unclear),
   `hearthroom-card-blueprint` or the narrow shape skill (structure missing),
   `hearthroom-originality-adapter` (renamed-copy risk remains),
   `hearthroom-card-author` (ready for fields) or
   `hearthroom-quality-auditor` (the author wants a score).

## Do not

- Do not copy sample names, scene text, voice lines, tag strings, route
  names, resource labels or schema labels.
- Do not attach origin or performance claims to samples; they are synthetic
  teaching fixtures.
